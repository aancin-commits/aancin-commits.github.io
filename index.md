# Kiro Contactos

**Private personal contact management application**

Kiro Contactos is a non-commercial application developed for personal use.

It synchronizes contacts between Google Contacts and Microsoft Outlook and maintains a private personal address book.

Contact information is stored on the owner's private NAS, with encrypted backups. The application is not offered as a public service.

The application uses Google Contacts permissions exclusively to read, create, update and synchronize contacts at the owner's request.

# Kiro Contactos — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 21, 2026**

## 1. About the application

Kiro Contactos is a private, non-commercial application used by its owner to manage and synchronize personal contacts between Google Contacts and Microsoft Outlook.

## 2. Information accessed

The application accesses contact information authorized by the owner, including names, telephone numbers, email addresses, postal addresses, birthdays and notes.

It does not request access to Gmail messages or Google Drive files.

## 3. How information is used

Contact information is used exclusively for personal contact management, synchronization, organization and enrichment with personal notes and relationships.

Information from Google Contacts may be synchronized with the owner's Microsoft Outlook account.

## 4. Storage and security

Contact information is stored on the owner's private NAS, protected by access controls. Encrypted backups may be stored in the owner's personal cloud storage.

The application is not a public service.

## 5. Data sharing

Contact information is not sold, used for advertising or shared with advertisers.

Information is transferred between Google Contacts and Microsoft Outlook only for the contact synchronization functionality authorized by the owner.

## 6. Data retention and deletion

The owner controls the stored information and may edit, archive or delete contacts. The owner can also revoke the application's Google access through Google Account security settings.

## 7. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the application owner at aancin@gmail.com.
